Based on learning research

Proven lines for lasting learning

Tarteel: Slow, clear, steady

The Quran itself asks us to recite in measured tones (73:4). Slow, clear recitation is the foundation of correct Tajweed.

Tajweed: Learn it right, recite it well

Feedback on pronunciation is one of the strongest influences on learning (Hattie, 2009). Every student is corrected in a small group.

Muraja'ah: Recall to remember

Actively reciting from memory strengthens recall more than rereading (Roediger & Karpicke, 2006). Daily sessions bring the rules back to mind.

Riyaadah: Little and often

Spaced practice is remembered longer than cramming (Cepeda et al., 2006). A short, steady hour each day builds lasting habits.
